
# E-Banking Service Quality and E-Customer Satisfaction

## Project Overview
This research investigates the relationship between electronic banking service quality and customer satisfaction among undergraduate students at the University of Ruhuna, Sri Lanka.

## Research Objectives
To examine the relationships between customer satisfaction and five e-banking service quality dimensions:
- Reliability
- Responsiveness
- Web Design
- Functionality
- Security and Privacy

## Dataset
- **Sample Size:** 104 undergraduate students
- **Data Source:** Primary data collected through a structured questionnaire
- **Research Approach:** Quantitative research

## Tools and Statistical Techniques
**Software:** IBM SPSS Statistics

**Methods:**
- Descriptive Statistics
- Pearson Correlation Analysis
- Cronbach's Alpha Reliability Analysis
- KMO and Bartlett's Tests
- Factor Analysis
- Hypothesis Testing

## Key Findings
The study identified positive correlations between e-banking service quality dimensions and customer satisfaction. The findings emphasize the importance of secure, reliable, and user-friendly digital banking services.

Some measurement scales showed relatively low internal consistency, indicating opportunities for improving the questionnaire in future studies.
